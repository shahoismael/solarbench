## 7. Headline claims and where each number comes from

**C1. Cross-climate generalization gap exists**

Every model: positive skill in-climate, negative transferred. MLP +0.055 -> -0.106; LSTM +0.066 -> -0.143; Transformer +0.063 -> -0.180

_Source: `phase6_transfer_matrix_results_tuned.csv`_

**C2. The gap is statistically significant**

Wilcoxon paired on site, significant in 9/9 tests, effect size r=+1.00 in every case (unanimous across sites; 300/300 on Ausgrid)

_Source: `phase8_significance_tests_tuned.csv`_

**C3. Transfer penalty magnitude**

MLP +12.3%, LSTM +14.4%, Transformer +16.6% mean nRMSE increase; worst +49.8%

_Source: `phase6_table_transfer_loss_tuned.csv`_

**C4. Persistence only wins at one step**

Skill rises monotonically with horizon in 12/12 combinations; pooled mean skill +0.052 at the shortest horizon vs +0.369 at 180 min

_Source: `phase9_horizon_results.csv`_

**C5. Rare-event degradation is universal**

60/60 transfer cells worse on rare events, and positive in 540/540 threshold settings for the cloud rule

_Source: `phase6_table_rare_in_transfer_tuned.csv, phase10_rare_sensitivity.csv`_

**C6. The effect is specific to ramps, not all extremes**

Cloud-transient rule median +103.4%; clipping rule median -31.3%

_Source: `phase10_rare_sensitivity.csv`_

**C7. Gradient boosting is the strongest one-step learner**

GBM beats all three neural baselines on DKASC and Ausgrid under identical features

_Source: `phase11_gbm_weather_results.csv`_

**C8. Weather helps unevenly, which justifies the common feature set**

PVDAQ -16.9%, DKASC -6.7%, HKUST +0.4% RMSE; Ausgrid has no weather at all

_Source: `phase11_gbm_weather_results.csv`_
